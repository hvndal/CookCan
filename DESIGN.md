# CookCan Design System

## Brand Identity
**Name:** CookCan
**Tagline:** "Scan. Cook. Enjoy."
**Theme:** Premium, modern, warm, and appetizing. Evokes premium home cooking, warmth, comfort, and inspiration. Avoid childish designs or overwhelming fast-food aesthetics.

## Color Palette
**Primary Palette:**
- Warm Amber
- Golden Yellow
- Soft Orange
- Burnt Orange
- Accent: Tomato Red (use sparingly for accents only)

**Backgrounds & Surfaces:**
- Warm Cream backgrounds
- Soft Charcoal text for high contrast and readability

## Typography
- Premium, modern, and sophisticated typography (e.g., Inter, SF Pro, or similar elegant sans-serif fonts).
- Large, bold headlines.
- Generous spacing.

## Shapes & Geometry
- Rounded cards and buttons for a friendly, modern feel.
- Clean lines and generous padding/margins.

## Iconography
- Use only modern icon systems.
- Preferred: Lucide or Phosphor Icons.
- **Rule:** Never mix icon libraries. Keep stroke weights consistent.

## Motion & Interactions (Conceptual)
- Spring animations, smooth transitions.
- Subtle gradients and haptic feedback.
- Fluid transitions and tasteful parallax.

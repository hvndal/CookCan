# CookCan Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-29 · Placeholder for Play Store. Host this page and set `extra.privacyPolicyUrl` in `app.json`._

CookCan (“we”) provides an ingredient-scanning and recipe app.

## Data we process
- **Account:** email if you sign in (Supabase) or a local guest session on device  
- **Pantry / recipes / grocery / meal plans / chat:** stored on device; may sync if cloud auth is enabled  
- **Photos / camera frames:** used to identify ingredients via Gemini; not used for ads  
- **Purchases:** handled by Google Play Billing; we store entitlement state on device  
- **Diagnostics:** basic crash/error logs on device

## How we use data
To provide scanning, recipes, AI Chef (Pro), subscription access, and app improvement.

## Sharing
- **Google Play** for subscriptions  
- **Google Gemini** for image/ingredient and recipe generation when an API key is configured  
- **Supabase** for optional authentication  

We do not sell personal data.

## Retention
Local data remains until you clear app storage or sign out. Cloud account data follows your Supabase project retention.

## Your choices
- Continue without account (local only)  
- Revoke camera/photos in system settings  
- Manage / cancel Pro in Google Play subscriptions  
- Contact: support@cookcan.app

## Children
CookCan is not directed at children under 13.
